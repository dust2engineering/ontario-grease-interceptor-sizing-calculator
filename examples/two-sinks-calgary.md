# Worked example 2 — two sinks (City of Calgary's published example)

Sink one 0.4 × 0.4 × 0.4 m = 64 L; sink two 0.3 × 0.3 × 0.3 m = 27 L.
One-minute drain down:

Q = 0.75 × (64 + 27) / 60 = **1.14 L/s = 68.25 L/min** → choose an interceptor
rated above 68.25 L/min. (Matches the City of Calgary handout exactly.)

Command: `python3 grease_interceptor.py 60 0 64 27`
