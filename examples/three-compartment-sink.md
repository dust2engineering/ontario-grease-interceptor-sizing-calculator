# Worked example 1 — three-compartment sink (Halifax Water's published example)

Three compartments, each 17 in × 17 in × 12 in: total volume 10,404 in³ =
170.5 L (45 US gal). One-minute drain down, no pump:

Q = 0.75 × 170.5 / 60 = **2.13 L/s = 127.9 L/min = 33.8 gpm** → select an
interceptor rated **35 gpm** or larger. (Halifax Water's fact sheet works this
same example to 34 gpm → 35 gpm unit.)

Command: `python3 grease_interceptor.py 60 0 170.5`
