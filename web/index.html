The rule

Sentence 7.4.4.3.(7) sets the flow through a grease interceptor as Q = Σ (0.75 × V ÷ DDT) + PD — each fixture's brim volume, factored to 75% (a fixture is filled to about 75% and contents displace the rest), divided by its drain down time, plus any pump discharge. The interceptor's rated capacity must not be exceeded by Q. Interceptors are selected and installed to CSA B481.3 and rated to CSA B481.1 or B481.2.
